Your request data

We store your answers, consent, and the submission time in a Cloudflare database in our account to review beta access and contact you about testing. We remove requests daily once they are 90 days old. For spam protection, we temporarily store a keyed hash of your IP address, not the address itself. Deleted records may remain in provider backups until those expire. Notifications contain a request ID, not your answers. There are no tracking scripts or outside form services.

To delete your request sooner, email {{ site.email }} from the address you submitted and ask to delete your beta request.

A short description is enough. Please leave out passwords and private data.
